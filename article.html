President Bio showcases Farms in Moyamba as Feed Salone Marks 3 years

By Sierra Leone News Media

October 19, 2026

President Dr Julius Maada Bio and First Lady Fatima Bio on Saturday concluded a day-long field tour of three major agricultural investments in Moyamba District, using symbolic harvests to showcase how private enterprise, women-led agribusiness and the military are driving his flagship Feed Salone initiative.

At the centre of the visit was Yoni, Kamajei Chiefdom, where President Bio, who is Commander-in-Chief, joined officers and men of the Republic of Sierra Leone Armed Forces (RSLAF) to harvest rice cultivated on 4,227 hectares.

The President said the three sites show the complete Feed Salone value chain from research, to mechanized production, to institutional market.

“This is what Feed Salone was designed for private sector at the forefront, connecting commercial farms with out-growers and creating jobs for young people,” he said, reaffirming government support through financing windows with the United Nations Capital Development Fund (UNCDF).
