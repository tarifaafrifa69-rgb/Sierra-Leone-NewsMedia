APC announces centers for Upcoming Elections

By Sierra Leone News Media

October 17, 2026

The All Peoples Congress (APC) National Secretariat has published the official list of centres where the party's District Elections will take place this Saturday, 26 September 2026.

In a notice dated 23 September 2026 and issued under the party's motto “Action Progress Commitment,” the Secretariat informed all party members, delegates, candidates, agents and stakeholders of the designated venues across the country.

The party urged comrades to take note of their assigned centres and make the necessary preparations.

Party members and stakeholders are advised to confirm their exact centres and prepare accordingly ahead of Saturday's polls.
